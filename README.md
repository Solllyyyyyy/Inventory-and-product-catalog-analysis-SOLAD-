# Inventory-and-product-catalog-analysis-SOLAD-

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a retail product dataset for **SOLAD**, a company offering a wide variety of products.

The analysis focuses on understanding the composition of the product catalog, inventory distribution, brand concentration, product availability, and low-stock categories.

The project follows a data storytelling approach:

**Dataset → Observations → Questions → Visuals → Insights**

---

## Dataset

* The dataset contains **10,000 product records** with information about each product's identity, category, pricing, stock, and availability.
* It also contains **9,000+ brands** spanning across **34 categories**

## Initial Observations

Before creating the visualizations, several characteristics of the dataset stood out:

* The catalog contains a large number of products across 34 categories.
* The dataset has over 9,000 unique brands, making direct brand-level analysis difficult to visualize.
* Stock and availability information provide an opportunity to examine inventory health.
* Categories provide a more manageable level for comparing product and stock distribution.
* Because of the high number of brands, the analysis uses a **Top 5 brand view** when examining brand concentration.

These observations helped determine which questions would be most useful to investigate.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis focuses on three main questions:

### 1. How is the product catalog distributed across categories?

This helps identify which categories contain the largest number of products and where the catalog is most concentrated.

### 2. Which brands have the highest number of products in the catalog?

A Top 5 brand analysis is used to identify the brands contributing the most products to the catalog.

### 3. Where are the main inventory and availability risks?

This looks at overall availability and identifies categories with the highest number of low-stock products, helping highlight potential restocking priorities.

---

## Visualizations

The dashboard contains five main visualizations:

### 1. Product Count by Category

Shows the number of products within each of the 34 categories.

**Purpose:** Understand how the product assortment is distributed across categories.

### 2. Total Stock by Category

Shows the total number of inventory units held within each category.

**Purpose:** Identify which categories contain the largest amount of stock.

### 3. Top 5 Brands by Product Count

Shows the five brands with the highest number of products in the catalog.

**Purpose:** Understand which brands contribute the most to the product assortment.

### 4. Availability Breakdown

Shows the distribution of products according to their availability status.

**Purpose:** Understand the overall availability of products in the catalog.

### 5. Low-Stock Products by Category

Identifies the categories with the highest number of products meeting the low-stock threshold.

**Purpose:** Highlight categories that may require greater restocking attention.

## Key Insights

The final insights are drawn from the dashboard visualizations and focus on:

* Categories with the largest product assortment
* Categories holding the most inventory units
* The brands contributing the most products to the catalog
* Overall product availability
* Categories with the highest number of low-stock products

These findings can help SOLAD better understand its product mix and prioritize inventory management decisions.

## Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how a large product dataset can be transformed into a focused business dashboard.

Rather than attempting to visualize all 10,000 products and 9,000+ brands individually, the analysis uses appropriate aggregation and selective Top N analysis to identify patterns that are easier to interpret and act upon.

The project demonstrates practical skills in:

* Data exploration
* Data aggregation
* Inventory analysis
* Category analysis
* Brand analysis
* Business question development
* Data visualization
* Data storytelling
* Presenting insights to a business audience
